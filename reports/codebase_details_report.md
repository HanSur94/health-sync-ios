## MAJOR

**1. The launchd job is not "unchanged in mechanism"; nothing hands it `HEALTH_SRC`.**
Plan (line 220): "unchanged in mechanism; only the source directory resolves differently through
`HEALTH_SRC`." The plist has no `EnvironmentVariables` key at all, and `HEALTH_SRC` exported in a
shell is not inherited by `launchd`. `launchctl list` shows `- 1 com.hannes.healthsync` — last run
exited 1 — and `~/Tools/health-data` (the script's default `SRC`) does not exist, so
`healthsync-nas.sh` hits its `[ -d "$SRC" ]` guard and exits 1. An implementer who follows step 5
("point the sync source at …") without editing the plist gets a silently failing daily job.
The earlier paragraph does mention "the launchd plist's environment or a one-line default change",
but the Files section states the opposite. Pick one and name the exact edit.

**2. "Verification already performed" no longer reproduces.**
Plan (lines 244–246) claims live `get_mcp_status` returned `ok:true` with data. Live call now:
`{"ok":false,"source":"file /opt/data/health/.health-cache.json","metricCount":0,
"workoutCount":0,"lastDataDate":null}`; `list_metrics` → `{"metrics":[]}`; `/opt/data/health` does
not exist on this machine. `node server.mjs --doctor` from `~/Tools/health-export-mcp` reports
"no cache file, metrics: 0, last data date: none". The specific numbers (schema 1, 6 metrics,
9 workouts) cannot be re-derived; no `export.xml`/`export.zip` remains on disk to re-run the
converter against. Acceptance step 6 currently fails. Either re-run the verification and restate
it, or mark it as historical with the date.

**6. The two copies of the schema already differ.** `apple-export-to-cache.py` `MAP` has 19 entries;
`SPECS` has 18. The extra is `sleep_duration_goal` (`HKQuantityTypeIdentifierSleepDurationGoal`),
which the app never writes. The "schema knowledge exists twice" statement holds, but the copies are
not in sync today, so "a change must be applied to both" understates the drift.

**7. "two files appear in the picked folder" (step, line 251) — they are dotfiles.**
The app writes `.health-cache.json` and `.health-workouts-cache.json`; Finder and the Files app hide
dot-prefixed names by default, so a correct first export looks like an empty folder. Verification
step should say "two hidden files (`ls -a`)".
